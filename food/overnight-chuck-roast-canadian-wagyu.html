Overnight Chuck Roast ("Canadian Wagyu")

Americandinnerroastedslow-cooked

Ingredients

  • 4-5 pound chuck roast
  • 2 tablespoons kosher salt
  • 2 teaspoons black pepper
  • 1 cup water (for the pan)
  • Wood chips of choice
  • Additional seasonings as desired

Instructions

Tap a step to highlight it

  1. ### Step 1: Initial Seasoning (3-4 hours before cooking)
  2. Pat chuck roast dry with paper towels
  3. Season generously with salt and pepper on all sides
  4. Let rest uncovered in refrigerator for 3-4 hours to allow seasonings to penetrate
  5. ### Step 2: Preheat
  6. Set oven to 200°F (93°C)
  7. Place rack in middle position
  8. ### Step 3: Optional Smoking Step
  9. If desired, smoke the seasoned roast for 2-3 hours at 200°F
  10. Use wood chips of choice
  11. ### Step 4: Oven Preparation
  12. Place roast in a roasting pan or deep baking dish
  13. Add 1 cup water to the bottom of the pan
  14. Cover tightly with foil
  15. ### Step 5: Slow Cook
  16. Place in preheated oven
  17. Cook for 10-16 hours depending on desired texture:
  18. 10-12 hours for firmer texture
  19. 14-16 hours for more tender result
  20. DO NOT exceed 16 hours as meat will become dry
  21. ### Step 6: Rest and Serve
  22. Remove from oven
  23. Let rest for 15-20 minutes
  24. Slice against the grain
  25. ## Sous Vide Alternative Method
  26. Set sous vide to 135°F (57°C) for medium-rare
  27. Vacuum seal seasoned roast
  28. Cook for 10-16 hours
  29. Optional: Sear in hot cast iron pan before serving
  30. ## Chef's Notes
  31. Aim for 10-16 hours cooking time; longer cooking will result in drier meat
  32. The meat should be tender but still sliceable
  33. Internal temperature will reach medium-rare while maintaining a tender, gelatin-rich texture
  34. Can be prepared before bed for next-day dinner
  35. Look for a well-marbled chuck roast for best results
