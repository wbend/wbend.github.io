German Pork Rouladen

1 hour 20 minutes1,200 cal total300 calories cal/serving
Germandinnerbakedporkspecial occasioncomfort food

Ingredients

  • 4 pork cutlets (scallopini)
  • 4 slices bacon
  • 1/3 white onion, diced
  • 1-2 dill pickles, diced
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 4 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups beef broth or stock
  • 1-2 tbsp grainy German mustard (such as fig-apple mustard)

Instructions

Tap a step to highlight it

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Spread mustard evenly over each of the 4 pork cutlets.
  3. Top each cutlet with one slice of bacon.
  4. Divide the diced onion and pickles among the cutlets, placing them at one end.
  5. Roll up each cutlet and secure with a toothpick.
  6. Place rouladens seam-side down in a baking dish lightly sprayed with cooking spray.
  7. In a small saucepan, melt butter over medium heat.
  8. Whisk flour into the melted butter to create a roux.
  9. Gradually whisk in beef broth and cook over medium-high heat until the mixture bubbles and thickens into a gravy.
  10. Remove saucepan from heat and pour gravy over the pork rouladens.
  11. Cover the baking dish and bake for 1 hour or until rouladens are cooked through and very tender.
  12. Serve over spätzle, egg noodles, rice, or potatoes of your choice.
