Easy Lemon Pepper Chicken

30 minutes840 cal total210 cal/serving
Americandinnerpan-searedgluten-free optionalquick weeknight mealunder 30 minuteslow-cost

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 1.3 lbs total)
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon pepper seasoning
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/8 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (optional)
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp butter

Instructions

Tap a step to highlight it

  1. Use a sharp knife to carefully fillet the chicken breasts into two thinner pieces (or use thin-cut chicken breasts).
  2. Combine the flour and lemon pepper seasoning in a bowl. Sprinkle the mixture over both sides of the chicken breast pieces and rub it in until the chicken is fully coated.
  3. Heat the cooking oil in a large skillet over medium heat. When the skillet and oil are very hot, add the chicken and cook on each side until golden brown (about 5 minutes per side). Remove the cooked chicken to a clean plate and cover to keep warm.
  4. Add the butter and minced garlic to the skillet and sauté for about one minute.
  5. Add the chicken broth to the skillet and whisk to dissolve all the browned bits from the bottom of the skillet. Add the lemon juice and allow the sauce to simmer for 3-5 minutes, or until it has reduced slightly. Taste the sauce and add salt if needed.
  6. Return the chicken to the skillet and spoon the sauce over top. Allow the chicken to heat through. Season with freshly cracked pepper and fresh chopped parsley (optional), then serve.
